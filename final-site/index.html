Finding 01 · Career Pathways

No single ladder.
Many different routes.

From humanities to engineering, agency to non-profit, solo IC to CDO — the paths here are as varied as the leaders themselves. What was once imagined as a ladder has become a lattice. The diversity of routes isn't an exception — it's the norm. Hover any node or flow to trace connections.

Finding 02 · Origins & Outcomes

The fork happens
earlier than you think

Your first professional context does more than start a career — it shapes the arc of it. Leaders who began freelance or independent are 9× more likely to eventually found a company. Those who started inside large design organizations show higher rates of reaching the executive track. The divergence happens before education, before title, before almost anything else.

Finding 03 · The Road Ahead

The tools are changing.
Everything else will too.

92% of design leaders expect significant changes to their tool stack over the next five years. But this isn't a software story — it's a structural one. When the tools that shape how we research, ideate, prototype, and deliver are evolving this quickly, the nature of design work itself begins to change. Leaders who once enforced process will need to guide principles. Teams that once scaled by headcount will need to deliver more with less.
