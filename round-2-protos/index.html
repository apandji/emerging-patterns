Finding 02 · Executive Track

The executive track takes
time and investment

Age, advanced education, and a corporate start are the strongest predictors of reaching VP, CDO, or Chief. Only 14% of this cohort made it — but the pattern is consistent across every dimension.

14.3% baseline executive rate across all 119 leaders
Finding 03 · Founder Track

Early independence breeds
later independence

Starting your career freelance or independent is the single strongest predictor of eventually founding. The fork happens at your very first job — before education, before level, before anything else.

9×
higher founder rate for freelance starters vs. agency starters
(37.5% vs. 4.3%, Fisher's exact p=0.025)
Finding 04 · Future Expectations

Everyone expects the tools to change.

"How much do you expect your tool stack to change in the next 5 years?" Nearly every leader said significantly — across every background, level, age, and region.
