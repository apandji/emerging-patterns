Finding 2 : Starting Points

Starting roles can shape the career.

Three findings from this data hold up to significance testing. They tell a single story: where you start shapes your management trajectory — but not whether you enter it.

Methodology notes

Sample: 119 design leaders (54 female, 63 male, 2 non-binary). 71% North America. Fielded 2026. All three findings use Fisher's exact test; significance threshold p < 0.05.

What was tested and held up: Management entry parity (p=1.0), VP attainment gap (p=0.0499), freelance-to-independent track (p=0.049). These three are the basis of this document.

What was tested and did not hold up: Career switchers vs. lifelong designers reaching VP+ (p=0.69 — no meaningful difference). Non-design backgrounds reaching senior levels (untested, effect size too small). These have been removed.

On the "9× founder rate" from the main site: Arithmetically correct under a narrow "founder" definition, but rests on n=3 freelance founders. The 7.2× figure here uses a broader independent-track definition (Partner, Advisor, Founder titles) against a larger baseline. Prefer this framing.

Freelance caveat: n=9. The freelance finding is significant but fragile — one person changing roles changes the percentages substantially. Flag as directional.
